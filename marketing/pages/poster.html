Capstone Project · JJ Furniture Shop and Enterprises · Valencia City
JJ Furniture Management System
One shared, live order record for a custom furniture workshop: from a customer's 3D design to the delivered piece, across five role-based portals.
The problem

Orders lived on paper and phone calls

  • Handwritten slips at the counter, easy to misplace
  • Workshop priorities passed on verbally
  • Customers calling again and again for status
  • Deliveries and collected cash hard to reconcile
Our solution

One live record · five portals · seven stages

  • Every role sees the same order, updated in real time
  • Customers design and track orders themselves
  • Production starts only after a verified 30% deposit
  • Works in any phone browser: nothing to install

Live 3D configurator
Type, design, size, wood and finish update a real-time 3D model beside a 165 cm human figure, so customers see true scale.
Quote by email, accept online
Official quotations arrive with a single-use review link. Customers confirm the spec AND the price, then create their own password.
30% production gate
No wood is cut until a 30% down payment is verified. Unverified submissions never count; the delivery fee stays separate.
Real-time live sync
One shared order record: a craftsman's tap updates the owner's dashboard and the customer's tracker instantly.
Offline-first driver app
Assignments are saved on the phone. Delivered + cash collected is stored locally and syncs automatically with an honest status.
Secure by design
Role-based access with Supabase Row-Level Security, atomic single-use links, encrypted email tokens, no plaintext passwords.
